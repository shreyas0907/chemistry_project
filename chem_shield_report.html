1. Executive Summary & Industrial Problem Definition

Accidental chemical mixing, unsegregated warehouse storage, and improper effluent routing in chemical processing facilities trigger catastrophic industrial disasters—including the evolution of lethal gases (such as Hydrogen Sulfide, Chlorine, and Nitrogen Dioxide), thermal runaway explosions in reactors, solvent flash fires, and toxic wastewater contamination.

CHEM-SHIELD provides a scientifically validated digital twin and decision-support engine. It bridges raw chemical safety data (PubChem, Safety Data Sheets, NOAA CAMEO, NIOSH) with practical industrial operations across four target manufacturing sectors:

  • Leather Tanning & Processing: Segregation of toxic sulfide beamhouse floats ($Na_2S, NaHS$) from acidic pickling floats ($H_2SO_4, HCOOH$) to eliminate fatal $H_2S$ gas evolution; trivalent chromium ($Cr(OH)SO_4$) precipitation to prevent environmental discharge.
  • Pharmaceutical API Manufacturing: Segregation of halogenated organic solvents ($DCM$), flammable extraction reagents ($IPA, THF$), aggressive acyl halides ($SOCl_2$), and cytotoxic drugs ($Cisplatin, Methotrexate$).
  • Cosmetics & Personal Care: Safe bulk storage of high-strength oxidizers ($50\% H_2O_2$), concentrated organic and inorganic acids, active surfactants ($SLES, CAPB$), and preservatives.
  • General Chemical Processing: Acid-base neutralization management, secondary containment berming, and bulk tank farm SCADA monitoring.

2. Architectural Overview of the 9 Core Platform Modules

Module 1: Command Center (SCADA Dashboard) LayoutDashboard

Real-time supervisory control room displaying active inventory metrics (55 formulary substances), verified CAMEO incompatibility counts (207 high-risk pairs), storage bay audit flags, high-risk carcinogen/PBT inventory, and waste stream routing. Includes live interactive Recharts bar charts and pie charts segmented by sector.

Module 2: 3D Industrial Complex (Three.js WebGL Digital Twin) WebGL Engine

A full 3D interactive virtual chemical facility rendered in Three.js featuring:

  • Bulk Tank Farm: 4 heavy industrial tanks ($H_2SO_4$, $NaOH$, $H_2O_2$, and $DCM$) with liquid level sight-tubes, floating hazard rings, and breather vents.
  • Segregated Warehouse Racks: Steel pallet racks stacked with 200L drums color-coded to GHS classes.
  • Process Reactor R-301: 5,000L jacketed Hastelloy reactor with motorized agitator, condenser column, and rupture disc piping.
  • ETP Treatment Basins: Diffused aeration basins with surface foam animation and clarifier impellers.
  • Raycasting & SCADA Telemetry Drawer: Clickable assets slide open real-time telemetry (temp, pressure, fill %, GHS warnings, and CAMEO group incompatibilities).
  • 5 Camera Presets: Overview, Warehouse, Reactor, ETP Plant, Emergency Station.
Module 3: Chemical Laboratory (Master Formulary) PubChem PUG REST

Full searchable library of 55+ industrial chemicals with instant search by chemical name, CAS Registry Number, or molecular formula. Direct live integration with NIH PubChem PUG REST API queries 2D/3D structures, molecular properties, and GHS hazard statements on demand.

Module 4: Chemical Compatibility Analyzer Engine CAMEO Reactivity Matrix

Rules-based deterministic compatibility engine using the authoritative NOAA CAMEO 26-group reactivity matrix. Evaluates pairwise combinations and enforces the Precautionary Principle: if empirical reactivity data is missing, the pair is flagged as UNKNOWN / INSUFFICIENT DATA (never assumed safe). Displays verified chemical equations and emergency actions.

Module 5: 3D Chemical Warehouse Segregation Planner NFPA 400 & TRGS 510

Interactive warehouse layout simulator with 6 isolated containment bays: Flammables (`BAY-A`), Inorganic Acids (`BAY-B`), Bases (`BAY-C`), Oxidizers (`BAY-D`), Toxics (`BAY-E`), and Sulfides (`BAY-F`). Computes dynamic Warehouse Safety Score (0–100%), simulates dangerous co-storage, and features an "Auto-Segregate (NFPA 400)" optimizer that eliminates all adjacent cross-reactivity.

Module 6: Industrial Emergency Response Trainer OSHA 1910.120 HAZWOPER

Interactive incident commander trainer with 6 disaster scenarios ($H_2S$ Gas Evolution, Solvent Flash Fire & BLEVE, Reactor Exothermic Runaway, Nitric Acid Tank Breach, Chromium Spill, Cytotoxic API Breach). Features 4-stage decision trees, live simulation clock, OSHA scoring algorithm (+25 / -20 pts), and exportable incident audit dossiers.

Module 7: 3-Stream Wastewater Treatment (ETP) Simulator Kinetic Modeling

Models the physics and chemistry of industrial wastewater treatment:

  • Stream A (Sulfide Beamhouse Float): Catalytic oxidation ($2S^{2-} + 2O_2 + H_2O \xrightarrow{MnSO_4} S_2O_3^{2-} + 2OH^-$) with aeration sliders and catalyst dosage.
  • Stream B (Chromium Tanning Float): Alkali precipitation with $MgO$ or $Ca(OH)_2$ at pH 8.8 ($Cr^{3+} + 3OH^- \to Cr(OH)_3 \downarrow$).
  • Effluent Compliance Radar: Evaluates simulated effluent against EPA/CPCB limits ($S^{2-} \le 2.0\text{ ppm}$, $Cr \le 2.0\text{ ppm}$, $COD \le 250\text{ ppm}$, $pH\ 6.5–8.5$).
Module 8: AI Chemical Risk Intelligence Deterministic + AI

Calculates 4 traceable risk indices (0–10): Fire & Flash Index, Toxic Inhalation/IDLH Index, Corrosivity/Exotherm Index, and Aquatic Ecotoxicity Index based on chemical identity, storage environment, temperature ($10–55^\circ\text{C}$), and ventilation mode. Generates an interactive hazard heatmap and mandatory engineering safeguards.

Module 9: Safety Reports & Compliance Analytics Audit Dossiers

Configures and generates downloadable compliance dossiers in PDF, JSON, and CSV formats covering compatibility matrices, segregation plans, emergency drill scores, and ETP effluent certificates.

5. Regulatory Compliance & Standards Traceability

Authoritative Standards Alignment: All chemical compatibility evaluations, storage segregation rules, and emergency response guidelines embedded within CHEM-SHIELD conform strictly to the following regulatory frameworks:
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1200: Hazard Communication Standard (GHS Rev 8 classification & SDS authoring).
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120: Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response (HAZWOPER incident command).
  • NFPA 400: Hazardous Materials Code (2022 Edition - Storage Bay Isolation Distances & Containment).
  • NOAA CAMEO Chemicals: Computer-Aided Management of Emergency Operations Reactivity Worksheet v2.8.
  • EPA Risk Management Plan (RMP): Clean Air Act Section 112(r) for offsite atmospheric toxic gas release modeling.
  • ECHA REACH Regulation (EC 1907/2006): Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals.
  • German AwSV: Ordinance on Facilities for Handling Substances Hazardous to Water (WGK 1, 2, 3 classifications).
